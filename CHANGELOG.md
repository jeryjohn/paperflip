## 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* `FlipBookWidget` — any Flutter widget as page content.
* `FlipBookPdf` — PDF rendering via `pdfrx ^2.4.4`.
* `FlipBookController` — `flipNext`, `flipPrev`, `goToPage`, `nextPage`, `previousPage`.
* Realistic `PageFlipPainter` with shadow, back-face gradient, and fold highlight.
* Hot-corner drag detection from all four corners.
* Portrait (single page) and landscape (double-page spread) auto-layout.
* Page indicator overlay.
